Dice tray — roll 2d6

Monster of the Week rolls are 2d6 + a rating. Tap a rating to roll with your hunter's modifier for it (from the ratings line you picked in setup). Use the situational adjuster for a +1 forward, +1 ongoing, or a −1, and Clear log to wipe the roll history. Rolls are not saved — the log lives only while this page is open.

    How saving works

    Every change is saved automatically to this browser (localStorage), under your hunter's name — so you can keep several hunters and come back to them. It does not sync to other devices or browsers. Use Export JSON to back a hunter up or move them to another device, and Import JSON to bring them back. Print Sheet prints only the finished sheet, with your picked moves, trackers, and notes. Edit setup takes you back to setup with everything kept — setup and sheet are two views of the same hunter, not two hunters.

    Veteran start (level 13 conversions): +1 to two ratings, two extra moves, three improvements (one may be advanced), luck starts at 6 of 7, plus a scar, a famed deed, and an old debt. Those fields are on every sheet.
