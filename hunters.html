Choose a playbook

The original 12, then the 16 new ones from the wider world. Picking a playbook changes the sheet below — your hunter's name and notes stay with the hunter, not the playbook.

How saving works

Every change is saved automatically to this browser (localStorage), under your hunter's name — so you can keep several hunters and come back to them. It does not sync to other devices or browsers. Use Export JSON to back a hunter up or move them to another device, and Import JSON to bring them back. Print Sheet prints only the sheet itself, with your picked moves, trackers, and notes.

Veteran start (level 13 conversions): +1 to two ratings, two extra moves, three improvements (one may be advanced), luck starts at 6 of 7, plus a scar, a famed deed, and an old debt. Those fields are on every sheet.
