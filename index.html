Dice tray — cast the bones

Cast the bones: every roll in this game is two six-sided dice + a rating. Tap a rating to roll with your hunter's modifier for it (from the ratings line you picked in setup). Use the situational adjuster for a +1 forward, +1 ongoing, or a −1, and Clear log to wipe the roll history. Rolls are not saved — the log lives only while this page is open.

    Your sheet keeps itself

    Every change is written into this browser's logbook as you go, under your hunter's name — so you can keep several hunters and come back to them. It does not travel to other devices or browsers. Use Export JSON to carry a hunter to another device, and Import JSON to bring them back. Print Sheet prints only the finished sheet, with your picked moves, trackers, and notes. Print Blank Sheet prints the chosen playbook's sheet with everything empty — blank name, look, gear, unticked moves and empty trackers — ready to fill in by hand; it never changes your saved hunter. Edit setup takes you back to setup with everything kept — setup and sheet are two views of the same hunter, not two hunters.

    Every hunter begins seasoned: +1 to two ratings, two extra moves, three improvements (one may be advanced), luck starts at 6 of 7, plus a scar, a famed deed, and an old debt. Those fields are on every sheet.
