Moves Reference

How to read a move: roll two six-sided dice and add the rating named. 10+ is a clean success. 7–9 succeeds at a cost, a compromise or a hard choice. 6 or less is a miss: mark experience, and trouble arrives. 12+ is an advanced result — but you only get a move's 12+ result if you have perfected that move; a 12+ on a move you haven't perfected counts as a 10+.

Hunter Agenda

  • Act like you're the hero in this story (because you are).
  • Make your own destiny.
  • Find this week's Pirate Lord and stop him.
  • Play your hunter like they're a real person.

Basic moves — in full

Perfecting a move: a move's 12+ result only applies if you have perfected that move — a 12+ on a move you haven't perfected counts as a 10+. You perfect a move by taking the "Perfect a move" improvement when you level up.

Persuade Someone (+Charm)

Once you have given them a reason, tell them what you want them to do and roll +Charm.

10+A normal person: they'll do it for the reason you gave them. If you asked too much, they'll tell you the minimum it would take for them to do it (or if there's no way they'd do it).
7–9A normal person: they'll do it, but only if you do something for them right now to show that you mean it. If you asked too much, they'll tell you what, if anything, it would take for them to do it.
MissMark experience, and trouble arrives — the Chronicler decides what.
12+A normal person: not only do they do what you want right now, they also become your ally for the rest of the voyage (or, if you do enough for them, permanently).

The Pirate Lord and his captains cannot normally be persuaded.

Act Under Pressure (+Cool)

When you act under pressure, roll +Cool.

10+You do what you set out to.
7–9The Chronicler is going to give you a worse outcome, hard choice, or price to pay.
MissMark experience, and trouble arrives — the Chronicler decides what.
12+You may choose to either do what you wanted and something extra, or to do what you wanted to absolute perfection.

Fight (+Tough)

When you get into a fight, roll +Tough.

7–9You and whatever you're fighting inflict harm on each other. The amount of harm is based on the established dangers in the game: you inflict the harm rating of your weapon, and your enemy inflicts their attack's harm rating on you.
10+Choose one extra effect:
  • you gain the advantage — take +1 forward
  • you inflict terrible harm (+1 harm)
  • you suffer less harm (−1 harm)
  • you force them where you want them
MissMark experience, and trouble arrives — the Chronicler decides what.
12+Instead pick an enhanced effect:
  • you suffer no harm at all
  • your attack inflicts double the normal harm
  • your attack drives the enemy away in a rout

Lend Aid (+Cool)

When you help another hunter, roll +Cool.

10+Your help grants them +1 to their roll.
7–9Your help grants them +1 to their roll, but you also expose yourself to trouble or danger.
MissMark experience, and trouble arrives — the Chronicler decides what.
12+Your help lets them act as if they just rolled a 12, regardless of what they actually got.

Guard Another (+Tough)

When you prevent harm to another character, roll +Tough.

7–9You protect them okay, but you'll suffer some or all of the harm they were going to get.
10+Choose an extra:
  • you suffer little harm (−1 harm)
  • all impending danger is now focused on you
  • you inflict harm on the enemy
  • you hold the enemy back
MissMark experience, and trouble arrives — the Chronicler decides what.
12+Both you and the character you are protecting are unharmed and out of danger. If you were protecting one of the port folk, they also become your ally.

Investigate (+Sharp)

When you investigate a voyage, roll +Sharp.

10+Hold 2.
7–9Hold 1.
MissMark experience, and trouble arrives — the Chronicler decides what.
12+You may ask the Chronicler any question you want about the voyage, not just the listed ones.

One hold can be spent to ask the Chronicler one of the following questions:

  • What happened here?
  • What sort of creature or foe is it?
  • What can it do?
  • What can hurt it?
  • Where did it go?
  • What was it going to do?
  • What is being concealed here?

Read a Bad Situation (+Sharp)

When you look around and read a bad situation, roll +Sharp.

10+Hold 3.
7–9Hold 1.
MissMark experience, and trouble arrives — the Chronicler decides what.
12+You may ask the Chronicler any question you want about the situation, not just the listed ones.

One hold can be spent to ask the Chronicler one of the following questions:

  • What's my best way in?
  • What's my best way out?
  • Are there any dangers we haven't noticed?
  • What's the biggest threat?
  • What's most vulnerable to me?
  • What's the best way to protect the victims?

If you act on the answers, you get +1 ongoing while the information is relevant.

Do Magic (+Weird)

When you use magic, say what you're trying to achieve and how you do the spell, then roll +Weird.

10+The magic works without issues: choose your effect.
7–9It works imperfectly: choose your effect and a glitch. The Chronicler will decide what effect the glitch has.
MissMark experience, and trouble arrives — the Chronicler decides what.
12+The Chronicler will offer you some added benefit.
Effects — choose one
Inflict harm (1-harm ignore-armour magic obvious).
Enchant a weapon. It gets +1 harm and +magic.
Do one thing that is beyond human limitations.
Bar a place or portal to a specific person or a type of creature.
Trap a specific person, cutthroat, or sea-beast.
Banish a spirit or curse from the person, object, or place it inhabits.
Summon a creature into the world.
Communicate with something that you do not share a language with.
Observe another place or time.
Heal 1-harm from an injury, or cure a disease, or neutralise a poison.
Glitches — choose one on a 7–9
The effect is weakened.
The effect is of short duration.
You take 1-harm ignore-armour.
The magic draws immediate, unwelcome attention.
It has a problematic side effect.

The Chronicler may say that… the spell requires weird materials; the spell will take 10 seconds, 30 seconds, or 1 minute to cast; the spell requires ritual chanting and gestures; the spell requires you to draw arcane symbols; you need one or two people to help cast the spell; you need to refer to a tome of magic for the details.

Big Magic

Use this when you want more than the Do Magic effects. Tell the Chronicler what you want to do. The Chronicler may require: you need to spend a lot of time (days or weeks) researching the magic ritual; you need to experiment with the spell — there will be lots of failures before you get it right; you need some rare and weird ingredients and supplies; the spell will take a long time (hours or days) to cast; you need a lot of people (2, 3, 7, 13, or more) to help; the spell needs to be cast at a particular place and/or time; you need to use magic as part of the ritual, perhaps to summon a creature, communicate with something, or bar the portal you opened; it will have a specific side-effect or danger. If you meet the requirements, then the magic takes effect.

Harm, armour, luck, recovery and levelling

Harm

When you suffer harm, ask the Chronicler what effect it has on you. Injury severity depends on how much harm you have suffered: 0-harm wounds have only minor, short-term effects. 4–7 harm wounds are serious and unstable. They will get worse unless treated — the sheet marks you Unstable automatically when you reach 4 harm; tap the line again to mark yourself stable once treated. 8-harm or more will kill a normal human, including a hunter. (Hunters track 7 harm boxes on their sheet; at 7, a hunter is dying.) Armour reduces the harm suffered by the number of points it is rated for.

Luck

When you spend a point of Luck, pick one: decrease a wound you have suffered to 0 harm; after you roll, retroactively change the result to a 12. When you have no luck left, bad things will happen to you. You have 5 luck. The track always shows 7 boxes — two boxes are already marked, luck spent in past adventures. You cannot earn more luck.

Recovery

  • 0-harm wounds are considered healed right away.
  • 1–3 harm wounds improve when you receive first aid, and later when you rest. Heal 1 when you do.
  • Unstable wounds require first aid to become stable. While unstable, they may get worse.
  • 4+ harm wounds require a healing move, time in an infirmary or hospital, or magical healing.
  • At the end of the voyage, you also have a chance to heal: if there is no chance to rest, heal 1 harm; if there is plenty of time, heal all harm.

Levelling up

Mark an experience point whenever your roll totals six or less, or when a move tells you to. Whenever you mark the fifth experience box, level up: tap Level Up on your sheet and choose one improvement from your playbook's list — one improvement per level, as per the rulebook. Your experience resets to 0 and your level goes up by 1. After you have levelled up five times, you may choose from the advanced improvement list as well.

End of session

At the end of each session, ask yourselves:

  • Did we conclude the current voyage?
  • Did we save someone from certain death (or worse)?
  • Did we learn something new and important about the world?
  • Did we learn something new and important about one of the hunters?

If you get one or two "Yes" answers, mark one experience. If you get three or four, mark two.
